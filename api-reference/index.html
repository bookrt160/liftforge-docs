    API reference¶

    Generated from the public headers of the Runtime module. Property types are in C++ spelling; the Blueprint display name is in brackets after an enum value.

    The tables list the public enums, structs, classes, properties, functions and events of the main Runtime headers, with their Blueprint access and category. Signage (ALFSignage) and the mirror capture (ULFMirrorCaptureComponent) are described in Presets and visual sets, and the AI classes in the NPC riding guide. The Demo and Editor modules (sample pawns, input config, Elevator Builder, asset library) are not part of the frozen API; their Blueprint-visible members also have tooltips. Not frozen: FLFPart, ELFPartRole and the structs of LFNetTypes.h are internal data that can change within 1.x.

    LFTypes.h (Runtime module)¶

    ELFElevatorType¶

    The purpose of an elevator: a label for your own logic. Only Panoramic changes the build (glazed cabin material). The Drive picks the machinery and the sound.

    Member Description
    Passenger (Passenger) Passenger car for people.
    Freight (Freight) Goods or service car with a high capacity and a plain cabin.
    Panoramic (Panoramic) Passenger car with glazed walls.
    Hydraulic (Hydraulic) Low-rise car lifted by a hydraulic ram.
    Platform (Platform Lift) Open accessibility platform lift.
    Cycle (Cycle Lift) Compact car sized for bicycles.

    ELFDrive¶

    The machine that moves the car. It decides which machinery is generated and which sound plays while moving.

    Member Description
    GearlessTraction (Gearless Traction) Traction machine without a gearbox.
    GearedTraction (Geared Traction) Traction machine with a gearbox.
    Hydraulic (Hydraulic) Hydraulic pump and ram.

    ELFDoorLayout¶

    How the cabin and landing doors open.

    Member Description
    CenterOpening (Center Opening) Two leaves that part at the centre.
    SideSliding (Side Sliding) One leaf that slides to the side.
    Telescopic (Telescopic) Several overlapping leaves that slide to the side.
    Hinged (Hinged) One swing leaf on a hinge.

    ELFElevatorState¶

    What the car is doing right now. Read it with Get State or listen to On State Changed.

    Member Description
    Idle (Idle) Parked or waiting for a call, doors closed.
    Opening (Doors Opening) The doors are opening.
    Open (Doors Open) The doors are fully open and the dwell timer runs.
    Closing (Doors Closing) The doors are closing.
    Accelerating (Accelerating) Moving: speeding up after departure.
    Cruising (Cruising) Moving: at full speed.
    Decelerating (Decelerating) Moving: slowing down towards the target floor.
    Leveling (Leveling) Moving: the last centimetres of the approach, creeping to the landing level.
    Stopped (Stopped) Held after a fault was reset or a trip was interrupted. Needs Resume Operation.
    Fault (Fault) A fault is active. See Get Fault.

    ELFElevatorMode¶

    The operating mode of a car. Anything other than Normal discards the queued calls.

    Member Description
    Normal (Normal) Automatic collective control: serves hall and cabin calls.
    Independent (Independent) Attendant service: serves cabin calls only, hall calls are rejected.
    Maintenance (Maintenance) Inspection service: no calls, the car moves only with Maintenance Jog.
    FireRecall (Fire Recall) Fire service phase 1: the car returns to the recall floor and opens its doors there.
    OutOfService (Out of Service) Out of service: the car stops at the next floor and accepts no calls.

    ELFFault¶

    A fault that can be injected into a car to test how a project reacts.

    Member Description
    None (None) No fault.
    DoorJam (Door Jam) The doors are jammed and cannot open or close.
    PowerLoss (Power Loss) Power loss: the car stops and the cabin lights go out.
    EmergencyBrake (Emergency Brake) The emergency brake dropped.
    StuckBetweenFloors (Stuck Between Floors) The car stopped between two floors.

    ELFDirection¶

    A travel or call direction.

    Member Description
    None (None) No direction: a cabin call, or a car with nothing to do.
    Up (Up) Towards higher floors.
    Down (Down) Towards lower floors.

    ELFElevatorCommand¶

    Everything a button, console, panel or Blueprint can ask an elevator to do. Pass one to Execute Elevator Command, the single entry point that the generated buttons, the operator console and the demo panel all use.

    Member Description
    None (None) No command. Unknown button names resolve to this and are ignored.
    SelectFloor (Select Floor) Cabin call: send the car to Floor Id.
    HallCallUp (Hall Call Up) Hall call going up from Floor Id. A linked bank picks the car.
    HallCallDown (Hall Call Down) Hall call going down from Floor Id. A linked bank picks the car.
    DestinationCall (Destination Call) Destination call from Origin Floor Id to Floor Id. A linked bank picks the car.
    OpenDoors (Open Doors) Open the doors at the current landing.
    CloseDoors (Close Doors) Close the doors.
    PassengerAlarm (Passenger Alarm) Sound the passenger alarm.
    EmergencyCall (Emergency Call) Emergency call button of the car operating panel. Raises On Command Executed only.
    Help (Help) Help button of the car operating panel. Raises On Command Executed only.
    Phone (Phone) Phone button of the car operating panel. Raises On Command Executed only.
    ToggleCutaway (Toggle Cutaway) Show or hide the cutaway of the shaft wall.
    ToggleExploded (Toggle Exploded View) Switch the exploded inspection view on or off. Switching it on puts the car into Maintenance.
    ToggleLabels (Toggle Part Labels) Show or hide the inspection labels of the parts.
    ToggleOverload (Toggle Simulated Overload) Simulate an overloaded cabin, or clear the simulated load.
    ToggleObstruction (Toggle Simulated Obstruction) Simulate a blocked door light curtain, or clear it.
    ModeNormal (Mode: Normal) Set the mode to Normal.
    ModeIndependent (Mode: Independent) Set the mode to Independent.
    ModeMaintenance (Mode: Maintenance) Set the mode to Maintenance.
    ModeFireRecall (Mode: Fire Recall) Set the mode to Fire Recall.
    ModeOutOfService (Mode: Out Of Service) Set the mode to Out Of Service.
    FaultDoorJam (Fault: Door Jam) Inject a door jam fault.
    FaultPowerLoss (Fault: Power Loss) Inject a power loss fault.
    FaultEmergencyBrake (Fault: Emergency Brake) Inject an emergency brake fault.
    FaultStuckBetweenFloors (Fault: Stuck Between Floors) Inject a stuck-between-floors fault.
    ResetFault (Reset Fault) Clear the active fault and hold the car until Resume.
    Resume (Resume) Release the hold after a reset and recover to the nearest floor.
    JogUp (Maintenance Jog Up) Maintenance jog to the next floor above.
    JogDown (Maintenance Jog Down) Maintenance jog to the next floor below.

    ELFCallRejectReason¶

    Why a call was not accepted. Reported by On Call Rejected and by Move Elevator To Floor.

    Member Description
    None (None) The call was accepted.
    UnknownFloor (Unknown Floor) The floor is not served by the car.
    SameFloor (Same Floor) The origin and the destination of the call are the same floor.
    InvalidDirection (Invalid Direction) A hall call needs Up or Down, and not Up at the top floor or Down at the bottom floor.
    AccessDenied (Access Denied) The access level of the caller is below the access level of the floor.
    Fault (Fault Active) A fault is active.
    ResumeRequired (Resume Required) A fault was reset and the car waits for Resume Operation.
    OutOfService (Out Of Service) The car is out of service.
    Maintenance (Maintenance Mode) The car is in Maintenance mode.
    FireRecall (Fire Recall Mode) The car is in Fire Recall mode.
    IndependentService (Independent Service) The car is in Independent mode, which accepts cabin calls only.
    InvalidConfiguration (Invalid Configuration) The configuration of the car is invalid, so it does not operate.
    NoElevatorAvailable (No Elevator Available) A bank found no car that can serve the call.

    ALFElevator¶

    One elevator: a car, its doors, buttons, displays, machinery and shaft, built from a preset or a configuration and operated by collective control. Every way to operate it goes through two entry points: Execute Elevator Command (doors, floors, modes, faults, inspection views) and Call To Floor (a trip between two floors). The generated buttons, the operator console, the demo panel and your own Blueprints all use them. Multiplayer: the server runs the car and replicates a compact state; clients rebuild the motion, doors, displays, sounds and events from it. On a client every command function is sent to the server through the local player's ULFElevatorInteractorComponent, and returns true when it was sent.

    Member Description
    TObjectPtr<ULFElevatorPreset> Preset
    read/write, Elevator|Configuration    		 Data asset that holds the configuration of this car. Used unless Use Configuration Override is on. Replicated: a change on the server rebuilds the car on every client.
    FLFElevatorConfiguration Configuration
    read/write, Elevator|Configuration    		 The configuration of this car, used when there is no preset or when Use Configuration Override is on. Replicated.
    bool bUseConfigurationOverride = false
    read/write, Elevator|Configuration    		 Use Configuration instead of the preset, so one car can differ from its preset. Replicated.
    TArray<FLFImportedPart> ImportedParts
    read/write, Elevator|Imported Assembly    		 When non-empty, RebuildAssembly places these source meshes instead of the generated kit geometry. ValidateConfiguration checks them alongside the configuration. Call MarkConfigurationDirty() or RebuildAssembly() after changing this from gameplay code. Replicated.
    FName ElevatorId = TEXT("A")
    read/write, Elevator|Identity    		 Short name of the car within its bank, for example A or B. Also used to break ties between cars. Replicated.
    TObjectPtr<ALFElevatorBank> DispatchBank
    read/write, Elevator|Identity    		 Bank that dispatches this car's hall calls. The bank lists the car as a member automatically. With World Partition prefer BankName (no hard actor reference). Replicated.
    FName BankName
    read/write, Elevator|Identity    		 Name of the bank this car belongs to. Cars and banks with the same BankName find each other at runtime through the world's LiftForge registry, so they can live in different World Partition cells.     LFElevatorBank.h (Runtime module)¶

    FLFBankCall¶

    A call the bank has assigned to a car, kept until the car serves it.

    ALFElevatorBank¶

    Dispatches hall and destination calls to a group of cars. A car belongs to a bank when the bank lists it, when the car names the bank in Dispatch Bank, when its Bank Name matches, or after Register Elevator. Membership is two-way: a car that names a bank is listed by it, and a bank that lists a car becomes the car's Dispatch Bank, so hall buttons always reach the bank. Multiplayer: the bank dispatches on the server only. On a client, Request Hall Call and Request Destination send the call to the server (through the local player's interactor) and return null; the requesting client then hears On Assigned or On Call Rejected for its own call.

    LFElevatorInteractorComponent.h (Runtime module)¶

    ULFElevatorInteractorComponent¶

    Sends a player's elevator requests (buttons, the operator console, UI commands, destination input) to the server in multiplayer. The server checks every request and uses its own Access Level for it; the client never decides that. You rarely call it yourself: on a client, Execute Elevator Command, Call To Floor, the command functions of ALFElevator, the bank's Request Hall Call and Request Destination and Press Button all route through the local player's interactor. LiftForge adds one to every player controller on the server (Project Settings > LiftForge > Multiplayer), or add it to your PlayerController or pawn. Call its functions directly for split-screen, where several local players share one machine.
