    Changelog¶

    1.0.0¶

    First release.

    Included¶

    • Elevator actor with generated cabin, doors, landings, shaft and machinery, built from a preset or a configuration; Elevator Builder editor tab.
    • Nine presets with visual sets and Blueprints: Traction, MRL630, MRL1000, Goods2000, Bed2500, Panoramic, Hydraulic, Platform and Cycle.
    • Collective control, bank dispatch and destination dispatch, with bounded waiting and registration by name.
    • Five operating modes and four injectable faults, with overload and door-obstruction handling.
    • Inspection views: cutaway, exploded view and part labels; operator console and demo panel.
    • A frozen Blueprint API: two entry points, a command enum, door, call-rejection, overload, obstruction and passenger events, status getters and the latent Move Elevator To Floor node.
    • Multiplayer: server-authoritative cars that replicate to listen-server and dedicated-server clients, client requests through an interactor component with server-side access checks, and riding tested under emulated latency and packet loss. Clients replay every elevator event in the server's order, even after packet loss: a client waits for a resend instead of skipping an event. See the Multiplayer guide.
    • NPC riding: Elevator Nav Links for a plain Move To across floors, Behaviour Tree and StateTree tasks, the latent AI Ride Elevator node, polite queueing, bank and destination dispatch. See the NPC riding guide.
    • Desktop sample pawn and an OpenXR VR sample pawn (not tested on a real VR headset), using Enhanced Input.
    • Art: 291 authored part meshes (every preset with its own cabin, doors, frames and lift-type parts; 3 LODs, collision per part), 19 CC0 surface materials and 3 procedural sets, with 14 master materials, signage actors (ALFSignage: screens, posters, slideshows, media textures) with 35 sample images, cabin screens, and a mirror capture component for real cabin-mirror reflections.
    • Example maps LF_Showcase_Warm and LF_Showcase_Noir: two looks of one building core with a glazed double-height lobby, a passenger bank, a service corridor, alcoves, landings and a plant corridor, lit with Lumen and an HDRI.
    • Source code for the runtime, AI, demo and editor modules, with automation tests.

    Behaviour you may notice¶

    • OnDoorsChanged(false) now fires after the state becomes Idle. Door events always follow the matching state change, so when a handler runs, the getters already show the new state. In 0.x builds the closed event fired first. If you used the event, check handlers that read the state.
    • Help, Phone and Emergency Call buttons raise On Command Executed and do nothing else; see Help, Phone and Emergency Call buttons.

    • A button with an unknown command name logs one warning and does nothing.

    • Elevator events now also fire on clients in multiplayer. Logic that must run once (NPCs, scoring, spawning) must check Has Authority.

    • The plugin enables the engine plugins StateTree and GameplayStateTree for NPC riding.
    • In multiplayer a character needs bStayBasedInAir; see Using your own character.
    • Out of Service lets passengers out: a moving car switched to Out of Service stops at the next floor, opens its doors once, closes them after the dwell and then accepts no calls.
    • In Maintenance mode the doors stay shut when a moving car stops at the next floor; Door Open still opens them.

    Versioning¶

    Within 1.x the names, parameter order and types of the documented API do not change, and enum values are only appended. New members are added with defaults. Removing or renaming a member is a major-version change.
