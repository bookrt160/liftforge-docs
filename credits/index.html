    Credits, licences and AI disclosure¶

    What is original¶

    The meshes, sound effects and the three procedural texture sets are original work made with scripts. 20 surface materials and 2 HDRI skies are CC0 (public domain) files from ambientCG and Poly Haven, processed for this plugin. The signage images are drawn by the author with fictional or generic names.

    Asset class How it was made
    Meshes Built from primitives with Blender Python. No scanned or downloaded geometry.
    Textures 19 CC0 surface materials (ambientCG, Poly Haven), resized and repacked. 3 procedural sets made by the author (numpy / PIL). 35 signage images drawn by the author.
    Sky 2 CC0 HDRI skies (Poly Haven).
    Materials 14 master materials and 105 material instances, authored in Unreal.
    Sound effects Synthesised (numpy / scipy), 48 kHz mono. Three MetaSound sources loop the motor, pump and door effects.
    Announcements AI-generated speech (see below).
    Fonts Noto Sans, Noto Serif (SIL OFL 1.1) and DejaVu (Bitstream Vera) were used to draw text into the signage images. No font file is shipped.
    Plugin icon Drawn by the author.

    Dimensions are catalogue-neutral and no manufacturer's product is represented.

    AI disclosure¶

    This product was created with the help of generative AI:

    • Spoken announcements. The ten short clips (floor names B2 to L4, "Doors opening", "Doors closing") are AI-generated audio. They were generated locally with the open-weight text-to-speech model Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0, https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) and its voice af_heart. The voice is a standard voice of the Kokoro model. No custom voice clone was made. The announcements are optional and replaceable.
    • Code and documentation. The source code and the documentation were written with AI assistance and reviewed by the author.

    Standards¶

    Passenger, goods, bed, panoramic and hydraulic cars stay within the EN 81-20 maximum car area for their rated load. Sizes are visualisation sizes, inspired by common ISO 4190-1 classes, and catalogue-neutral. The platform and cycle lift sizes are illustrative. Check the standard and your jurisdiction for real projects. LiftForge is not affiliated with and not endorsed by any standards body.
