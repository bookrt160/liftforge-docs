    Bank and destination dispatch¶

    A bank (LFElevatorBank) dispatches hall and destination calls to a group of cars. A single car without a bank runs its own collective control.

    Set up a bank¶

    1. Place an LFElevatorBank actor in the level.
    2. Link the cars. A car belongs to a bank in any of these ways, and they can be combined:
      • the bank lists the car in its Elevators array;
      • the car sets Dispatch Bank to the bank;
      • the car's Bank Name matches the bank's Bank Name;
      • you call Register Elevator on the bank at runtime.
    3. The link is two-way and automatic at BeginPlay. A car that names a bank is registered with it, and a bank registers every car it lists, so the car's hall buttons always reach the bank. Your authored Elevators array is never edited.
    4. Optionally place an LFOperatorConsole with the bank and an origin floor to get a destination keypad.

    Check the setup with Validate Bank. In the editor the same checks appear in Map Check. It reports a bank with no cars, a car that belongs to another bank, a Bank Name conflict, two cars with the same ElevatorId, and a car that shares no floor with the others.

    Hall calls¶

    Request Hall Call (Origin Floor Id, Direction) on the bank returns the car with the shortest arrival estimate, or null and raises the bank's On Call Rejected. Calls are re-checked every 0.1 s: when the assigned car gets a fault, leaves Normal mode, leaves the bank, or is no longer a member, the call moves to the best remaining car, or waits unassigned if there is none.

    Destination calls¶

    Request Destination (Origin Floor Id, Destination Floor Id) picks a car, calls it to the origin and selects the destination when its doors open there. Matching journeys are grouped: a car that already carries a call with the same origin and destination gets Destination Grouping Bonus Seconds (default 15) taken off its estimate.

    How calls are served¶

    Within one car, collective control de-duplicates cabin and directional hall requests and serves calls in the direction of travel first. Two settings keep that fair:

    • Starvation Seconds (default 30): a call older than this is served on the next trip.
    • Direction Change Penalty Seconds (default 10): added to a moving car's arrival estimate when it must reverse to serve a hall call.

    A tie between cars is broken by the stable ElevatorId.

    Access levels¶

    Each floor has an Access Level. A call needs a caller level at or above the level of both the origin and the destination floor. AccessLevel is a parameter of the calls; the demo cabin buttons use level 0 and the operator panel uses 9. Your game decides which level a player has.

    Why was my call refused?¶

    A refused call raises On Call Rejected with a reason (ELFCallRejectReason). Get Last Call Rejection returns the reason of the last call (None after an accepted one).

    Reason Meaning
    Unknown Floor The car does not serve that floor.
    Same Floor Origin and destination are the same.
    Invalid Direction A hall call needs Up or Down, and not Up at the top or Down at the bottom.
    Access Denied The caller's level is below the floor's level.
    Fault Active A fault is active.
    Resume Required A fault was reset and the car waits for Resume.
    Out Of Service / Maintenance Mode / Fire Recall Mode The car is in that mode.
    Independent Service The car is in Independent mode and takes cabin calls only.
    Invalid Configuration The configuration is invalid, so the car does not operate.
    No Elevator Available The bank found no car for the call (bank only).

    Wait for a car in Blueprints¶

    Move Elevator To Floor (see Blueprint API) sends a car to a floor and waits. With a bank, use Request Destination on the bank so the bank picks the car.

    Bank and World Partition¶

    Use Bank Name instead of hard actor references when the cars and the bank live in different World Partition cells. See World Partition notes.
