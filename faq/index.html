    FAQ and troubleshooting¶

    Setup¶

    I do not see any LiftForge assets. In the Content Browser open Settings and turn on Show Plugin Content. Also check Edit > Plugins that LiftForge is enabled.

    The plugin does not load on my engine version. Use the download that matches your engine (5.6, 5.7 or 5.8). Plugin content saved in a newer engine does not open in an older one, so LiftForge content is authored in 5.6.

    An example map is dark. The example maps have a post-process volume with manual exposure. A level without one uses auto exposure and can render very dark. Add a post process volume (unbound) and set the exposure to suit your scene.

    Cars do not behave¶

    The car does not move when I press a button. Check, in this order:

    1. Validate the configuration (see Elevator Builder). An invalid car does not operate, and the problem is in the Message Log.
    2. The mode. Anything other than Normal discards calls, and a hall call is refused in Independent mode.
    3. A fault: Reset it and then Resume Operation.
    4. Overload: remove the extra load or Toggle Overload.
    5. Access level: Get Last Call Rejection gives the reason. See Bank and destination dispatch.

    A custom button does nothing. Its command name is not in the list. The Output Log shows one warning for it. Use Set Command Type. See Help, Phone and Emergency Call buttons.

    Help, Phone and Emergency Call buttons do nothing. That is by design in 1.0. They raise On Command Executed. See Help, Phone and Emergency Call buttons.

    The doors reverse again and again. Something blocks the light curtain. Move it, or check Simulated Obstruction.

    My Move Elevator To Floor node never finishes. It finishes on Arrived or Failed. Set a timeout so it cannot wait for ever, and check the Failed output: a mode change, a fault or a rejected call ends it.

    My bank logs "no loaded cars". The cars are not linked or not loaded yet. See Bank and destination dispatch and World Partition notes.

    Riding¶

    The camera shakes in the car (jitter). Single-player checks:

    • Ride on the cabin floor with a standard Character, or add LFRiderComponent to a pawn without CharacterMovement.
    • Do not teleport or move the character yourself while it stands in the car.
    • Do not rely on moving the elevator actor itself at runtime if Static Fixed Parts is on (it makes the root Static).

    Multiplayer checks:

    • Turn on bStayBasedInAir on the CharacterMovement component and apply the other settings in Using your own character.
    • Do not move the car to a later tick group or drive it from a Timeline.
    • Do not change MaxWalkSpeed on the server and the client independently (for sprint, use a custom move flag).
    • Save the map before multiplayer Play In Editor.

    My character falls through the car floor.

    • Check that the character's collision blocks the generated cabin parts (see Using your own character).
    • Leave Shaft Collision on so the character cannot enter the shaft from the side.

    • For a pawn without CharacterMovement, add a LFRiderComponent and give the pawn a collidable root.

    • In multiplayer, turn on bStayBasedInAir on the CharacterMovement component. A jump inside a moving car then stays relative to the car. See Using your own character.

    My VR pawn falls through the car. Add a LFRiderComponent, use a collidable root, and keep the camera and controllers below a tracking-origin component. See VR setup.

    Packaging and cooking¶

    Which assets are packaged? The assets that LiftForge needs without a preset pointing at them are soft references in DefaultLiftForge.ini, and the cooker packages them automatically. You do not need DirectoriesToAlwaysCook. The BP_LF_* Blueprints, presets and visual sets are packaged when something you ship references them.

    Parts look wrong or use the default material in a packaged game. Instanced parts need materials with Used with Instanced Static Meshes enabled. All LiftForge materials have it. If you replaced a material, enable the flag on it. Cars fall back to ordinary components if any material in a group lacks it, so a missing flag costs performance but not correctness.

    Parts show "unbuilt lighting". You turned on Static Fixed Parts in a project with static lighting. Build the lighting, or turn the option off.

    Which platforms? Win64.

    Audio¶

    How do I remove or replace the announcements? Clear the floor's Announcement slot and the Doors Opening / Doors Closing slots of the audio pack, or assign your own sound waves. The announcements are AI-generated; see Credits, licences and AI disclosure.

    The sound is too loud or too quiet. Configuration.Audio.Volume (0.35 in the presets) scales the whole mix of one car. Apply Sound Mix in the LiftForge project settings controls the sound mix the cars push while they play.

    Other¶

    Can I use LiftForge for a real lift design? No. It is a visualisation and simulation of behaviour, not a certified control system, and car sizes are visualisation sizes. See the note on the Overview.

    Multiplayer: clients cannot use the cars. Save the map first: a level that was never saved cannot replicate level actors. Then check the player's interactor component and access level. See the Multiplayer guide.

    Another player's character pops by the crouch height in a moving car (multiplayer). With packet loss, Unreal's character movement can show a remote player's crouch height before the capsule changes, for about one round trip. It corrects itself. See Multiplayer guide.

    My game logic runs once per player when a door opens. Elevator events now fire on clients too. Check Has Authority before gameplay or NPC logic.

    NPCs do not ride. See the NPC riding guide.
