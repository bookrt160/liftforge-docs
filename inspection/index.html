    Inspection views¶

    Inspection views show how a lift is built. Use them from the operator panel, from the physical operator console beside each car, or from Blueprints.

    View Command What it does
    Cutaway Toggle Cutaway Hides the shaft wall on the side of the viewer so the car can be seen travelling. On by default (bCutaway).
    Exploded view Toggle Exploded View Moves the parts apart (ExplodedAmount from 0, assembled, to 1, fully exploded). Switching it on puts the car into Maintenance. Leaving Maintenance closes it.
    Part labels Toggle Part Labels Shows a text label on every part (bShowPartLabels). Labels are created the first time they are used.

    From Blueprints call Toggle Cutaway, Toggle Exploded, Toggle Labels, or Execute Elevator Command with ToggleCutaway, ToggleExploded or ToggleLabels.

    Operator console and panel¶

    The LFOperatorConsole actor is a wall of physical buttons for one car: modes, faults, recovery, the inspection views, plus a floor or destination keypad and a status line. Assign its Elevator, optionally its Bank (destination calls then go through the bank) and the Origin Floor Id it stands on. Use Rebuild Console after you change it. The demo's UMG panel (key H) offers the same commands in the same order.

    Camera enclosure tag¶

    The example maps hide lobby walls, lintels and ceilings in their inspection cameras by the actor tag LiftForgeInspectionEnclosure. Tag your own walls with it if you build review cameras that should look into the building.

    Tips¶

    • The exploded view moves static parts too. If you build with Static Fixed Parts, the exploded view switches them to Movable.
    • Cutaway hides geometry, not collision. For a walk-through leave Shaft Collision on so nobody walks into the shaft.
