    Operating modes and faults¶

    Modes¶

    Set a mode with Set Operating Mode, or with the Mode... commands of Execute Elevator Command. Any mode other than Normal discards the queued calls.

    Mode What the car does
    Normal Automatic collective control: serves hall and cabin calls.
    Independent Attendant service: serves cabin calls only. Hall calls are rejected (Independent Service).
    Maintenance Inspection service: no calls. The car moves only with Maintenance Jog (next floor up or down), starting with the doors closed. The doors open at the end of a jog, the only Maintenance stop where they open on their own.
    Fire Recall Fire service phase 1: the car returns to the recall floor and opens its doors there. Normal calls are cleared.
    Out of Service The car accepts no calls. A moving car stops at the next floor it can reach and opens its doors once so passengers can get out.

    Changing the mode while the car moves¶

    A moving car does not stop dead. Maintenance and Out of Service brake the car to the next floor it can stop at within its acceleration limit, with no jump in velocity. Fire Recall does the same when the car is moving away from the recall floor, keeps the doors shut on the way, then returns to the recall floor and opens its doors.

    In Maintenance the car stops at the next reachable floor and keeps its doors closed. The Door Open button and command still open them, so nobody is trapped. In Out of Service, a car that was moving stops at the next reachable floor and opens its doors once, so passengers can get out. It closes them after the normal dwell time and then accepts no calls. While those doors are still open or closing, Door Open and the door sensor reopen them as usual; once they are fully shut, Door Open is refused until the car leaves Out of Service. A car that is already standing still when switched to Out of Service is unchanged. Fire Recall is as described above.

    Faults¶

    Faults let you test how your project reacts. Start one with Inject Fault or the Fault... commands.

    Fault Effect
    Door Jam The doors cannot open or close.
    Power Loss The car stops and the cabin lights go out.
    Emergency Brake The emergency brake dropped.
    Stuck Between Floors The car stopped between two floors.

    A fault discards the queued calls and stops the car. While any fault is active, door commands are rejected. Reset Fault clears the fault and holds the car (state Stopped). While the car is held, Door Open is also refused. Reset never restarts travel by itself: call Resume Operation to continue. At a floor the car carries on; between floors it travels to the nearest floor. Reset Fault does nothing when there is no fault, and Resume Operation does nothing when the car is not held.

    Overload and door obstruction¶

    • Overload: the load of the cabin is the sum of the actors inside it. A pawn counts Passenger Mass Kg (75 by default) unless it has a LFLoadComponent with its own mass. Other actors count through that component or their physics-body mass. Above the capacity the car does not travel, and On Overload Changed fires. Toggle Overload simulates a load one passenger above the capacity.
    • Door light curtain: something in the doorway blocks the doors. Closing doors reverse and reopen, and On Obstruction Changed fires. Toggle Obstruction simulates a blocked curtain. The curtain is only read while the doors are not fully closed or while something is bound to the event.

    Status and events¶

    Read the state with Get State, Get Operating Mode and Get Fault, or bind to On State Changed, On Mode Changed and On Fault Changed. Events fire in a fixed order; see Blueprint API.

    Not a certified controller¶

    These behaviours illustrate how lifts react. They are not a certified control-system model. In particular fire service, inspection and fault handling are simplified for visualisation.
