    Presets and visual sets¶

    A preset (DA_Preset_<Key>) stores one elevator configuration so many cars can share it. A visual set (DA_VisualSet_<Key>) maps each part role (cabin wall, door frame, handrail and so on) to an authored mesh and its materials. A Blueprint (BP_LF_<Key>) is an elevator actor with the preset already assigned.

    All three exist for these nine keys: Traction, MRL630, MRL1000, Goods2000, Bed2500, Panoramic, Hydraulic, Platform, Cycle.

    Asset Path
    Presets /LiftForge/Presets/DA_Preset_<Key>
    Visual sets /LiftForge/VisualSets/DA_VisualSet_<Key>
    Blueprints /LiftForge/Blueprints/BP_LF_<Key>
    Example maps /LiftForge/Examples/Maps/LF_Showcase_Warm and /LiftForge/Examples/Maps/LF_Showcase_Noir

    Sizes¶

    Passenger, goods, bed, panoramic and hydraulic cars stay within the EN 81-20 maximum car area for their rated load. Sizes are visualisation sizes, inspired by common ISO 4190-1 classes, and catalogue-neutral. The platform and cycle lift sizes are illustrative. Check the standard and your jurisdiction for real projects. Lengths are in centimetres. The sizes do not represent any manufacturer's product.

    Preset Rated load Car W x D x H Door W x H Drive, doors
    Traction 800 kg, 10 persons 135 x 140 x 230 90 x 210 gearless traction, centre opening
    MRL630 630 kg, 8 persons 110 x 140 x 220 80 x 200 machine-room-less traction, side sliding
    MRL1000 1000 kg, 13 persons 160 x 140 x 230 90 x 210 machine-room-less traction, centre opening
    Goods2000 2000 kg 180 x 230 x 240 140 x 210 geared traction, telescopic
    Bed2500 2500 kg 200 x 240 x 240 130 x 210 gearless traction, telescopic
    Panoramic 1000 kg, 13 persons 150 x 150 x 250 90 x 210 gearless traction, centre opening, glazed walls
    Hydraulic 1000 kg 110 x 210 x 230 90 x 210 hydraulic, side sliding
    Platform 400 kg 110 x 140 x 210 90 x 190 hydraulic, hinged gate
    Cycle 500 kg 100 x 220 x 210 80 x 190 hydraulic, hinged gate
    • All presets use a 1.5 s door cycle.
    • The Platform speed is illustrative.
    • The MRL presets show a machine-room-less drive: the gearless machine sits on the rail tops and the controller is a cabinet beside the top landing door.
    • On the Platform and Cycle lifts, the landing gate opens into the car; keep the gate area clear. An outward-opening gate is planned for a later update.

    Every preset's cabin is authored at its exact size (walls, floor, ceiling, light, jambs, header, doors, handrails, sill, frame, toe guard and car operating panel cassette), each with its own design language: Traction walnut and bronze with a rear mirror; MRL630 oak and satin stainless; MRL1000 graphite laminate, brass trim and an LED cove; Goods2000 steel liner panels, bumper rails and a chequer floor; Bed2500 white HPL, vinyl floor, wide mirror and a fold-down seat; Panoramic faceted glass walls in a steel frame with a spotlight ring and a glazed hoistway; Hydraulic painted steel; Platform half-height powder-coated walls with a control column and a folding access ramp (lowered while the gate is open); Cycle rubber-lined walls with a wheel channel in the floor. The car operating panel sits on the right side wall next to the front on all nine presets (their return panels are narrower than 40 cm); a car with a return panel of 40 cm or more keeps it beside the door.

    The demo building used by the example maps has the floors B2 (0 cm), B1 (320), G (680), M (950), L1 (1250), L2 (1620), L3 (1980) and L4 (2360). Each preset serves a subset of them.

    Use a preset¶

    • Drag a BP_LF_<Key> into the level; or
    • add an LFElevator actor and set Preset to a DA_Preset_<Key>; or
    • pick it in the Elevator Builder.

    To make a variant, either create your own preset asset (a LFElevatorPreset data asset) or turn on Use Configuration Override on one car.

    Configuration fields: Shaft Material (a glass instance makes a glazed hoistway, as the panoramic preset uses), Cabin Screen (none, ceiling corner, side wall) with Cabin Screen Image, and Landing Wall Offset (how far the finished landing wall stands in front of the door plane; the hall station, lanterns and indicators are mounted on that face; 0 keeps the 1.0 layout). Mirror Capture gives the rear mirror panel a real reflection (a scene capture from the viewer's reflected position, one capture per frame while someone in front of it can see it: the doors are open, the viewer is inside the car, or the cabin is glazed); it needs the project setting Support global clip plane for Planar Reflections and is on in the Traction, MRL1000 and Bed2500 presets. Its edge smoothing relies on the main view's TSR or TAA (a sub-texel jitter on the capture that the temporal filter resolves); with FXAA, MSAA or no anti-aliasing the reflection is sharper but its edges are less smoothed. The mirror capture is untested in VR (see VR setup).

    Materials and finishes¶

    Master materials are in /LiftForge/Materials/Masters: M_LF_Metal, M_LF_PowderCoat, M_LF_Wood, M_LF_Stone, M_LF_Fabric, M_LF_Glass, M_LF_Screen, M_LF_Poster, M_LF_Foliage, M_LF_Emissive, M_LF_Display, M_LF_Surface, M_LF_SkyDome and M_LF_MirrorCapture. Every finish is a material instance in /LiftForge/Materials/Instances (MI_LF_*). To change a finish, edit the instance:

    Master Parameters
    Textured masters (metal, powder coat, wood, stone, fabric) BaseColor, Normal, ORM, Tiling (metres per repeat; 1 = 100 cm), UVRotation, Tint, TintStrength, RoughnessMin, RoughnessMax, NormalStrength, DetailNormal, DetailTiling, DetailStrength; metal adds Anisotropy, BrushAngle; fabric adds FuzzStrength
    M_LF_Surface Tint, Metallic, Roughness
    Emissive and display Color, EmissiveStrength
    M_LF_Glass Tint, Opacity, Roughness, IOR, FresnelExponent, SmudgeMask, SmudgeStrength
    M_LF_Screen ScreenImage, Brightness, PixelCount, GridStrength, FlipU, FlipV
    M_LF_Poster PosterImage, PaperRoughness, GlossStrength, FlipU, FlipV

    On the textured masters, TintStrength fades the Tint colour in: at 0 (the default) you see the scan as shot and Tint has no effect, so set TintStrength above 0 (1 = full tint) to see a new colour.

    Finish variants you can swap: woods Walnut, Oak, SmokedOak; stones Terrazzo, Travertine, TravertinePolished, Marble, StoneTile; metals Steel, Satin, Brass, Bronze, PanelSteel, LinerSteel, DarkMetal, Chequer; carpets Carpet, CarpetDark. Set a different instance in the visual set's Material Overrides for the slot you want to change.

    The mesh material slots keep their authored names (M_HQ_Steel, M_HQ_Walnut and so on). A visual set maps each slot to an instance. All master materials have Used with Instanced Static Meshes enabled, so material instances can be used on the instanced parts of tall buildings.

    Meshes¶

    • 291 part meshes (117 shared roles, 174 preset-specific parts) and 8 kit meshes. 30 of the shared meshes (for example SM_HQ_CabinDoor and SM_HQ_CabinFloor) are kit parts for your own visual sets: the presets and the example maps do not use them.
    • Each part mesh has 3 LODs: LOD0 authored in Blender, LOD1 (50 % of the triangles) and LOD2 (about 18 % on most meshes, up to 50 % on small parts) generated in Unreal. Each part has simple collision as authored (box; convex for the faceted and half-height walls; none for thin decorative parts, which then use their triangles) and a lightmap UV channel (coordinate index 1). Triangles over all unique part meshes: LOD0 728,333, LOD1 364,169, LOD2 131,695.
    • UVs are unwrapped at a real-world scale: 1 UV unit = 100 cm, so every CC0 surface material tiles at its physical size.
    • The 8 kit meshes are 1 m primitives with automatic collision and no LODs (188 or 764 triangles).
    • Decorative parts follow the configuration's Detail Draw Distance (8000 cm by default; 0 disables culling). Collision on the cabin floor, walls and doors stays available at any distance.
    • Nanite is not required.

    Signage¶

    LiftForge Signage actors (ALFSignage) show an image on any mesh whose image faces use the slots M_HQ_Screen (emissive LCD) or M_HQ_Poster (print). Choose a shipped mesh (ceiling-corner and wall cabin screens, lobby totem, landing screen, menu board, A1 and A2 poster frames, light box, directory board, wall sign) or keep your own mesh with Custom. Screen Image and Poster Image take any texture (35 samples ship: T_LF_Screen_<Name>, T_LF_Screen_<Name>_P portrait, T_LF_Poster_<Name>), Slides with Seconds Per Slide run a slideshow, and Media Texture shows a Media Framework texture (video) on the screen. Set Screen Image, Set Poster Image and Show Slide are Blueprint-callable. Nine of the sample images are not used by the example maps; they are extras for your own scenes.

    Textures and audio¶

    • 62 surface maps in 22 sets (19 CC0 surface materials from ambientCG and Poly Haven, 3 procedural), base colour / DirectX normal / packed AO-roughness-metallic; walnut, oak, stone tile, terrazzo and steel at 4096 x 4096, the rest at 2048 x 2048. 35 signage images. Two HDRI cubemaps (T_LF_HDRI_PlacWolnosci and T_LF_HDRI_NeuerZollhof, Poly Haven, CC0), one per showcase look, for the sky light and sky dome.
    • 19 sound waves (9 effects, 10 spoken announcements) and 3 MetaSound sources (motor, pump, doors).
    • Replace any sound by assigning your own sound wave in the configuration's audio pack, or clear the slot. Announcements are optional: leave the floor's Announcement slot and the Doors Opening / Doors Closing slots empty for chime-only operation.
    • Configuration.Audio.Volume sets the overall volume of one car (0.35 in the presets).

    Lighting of the example maps¶

    All maps use dynamic Lumen lighting. There is nothing to bake.

    Known limits of the example maps¶

    • Steel doors and panels in the cabins show cloudy reflections under software Lumen. Hardware Lumen removed them in our test, but took roughly two to three times the frame time.
    • The tower seen from the street is a plain block without windows, and a thin dark vertical band runs down its east wing.
    • The kerb joints in the forecourt read as a saw-tooth edge.
    • The Traction hero laylight shows one bright dot and two brighter patches.
    • The metal landing sills show dark blotches in close-up.

    Make your own visual set¶

    Create a ULFVisualSet data asset. Its Part Meshes map assigns a mesh to each role name (for example CabinWall or DoorFrame), with the mesh's authored size (Source Dimensions). The builder scales each part from that size to the size your configuration needs. The additive roles (MRLMachine, MRLController, HallLantern, LandingIndicator, CabinScreenCorner, CabinScreenWall, FoldSeat, Ramp, ControlColumn, GlassFrame, SpotRing, WheelChannel) are placed only when your set binds them. Set the Visual Set on a preset or a car. Leave it empty to build the car from the generated kit instead.
