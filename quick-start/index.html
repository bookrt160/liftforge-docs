    Quick start (5 minutes)¶

    You will place a ready-made elevator in a new level and ride it.

    Option A: look at an example map first¶

    First check that LiftForge is switched on: Edit > Plugins, search for LiftForge, tick Enabled, restart the editor. It is not switched on by itself (see Install).

    1. Enable Show Plugin Content in the Content Browser.
    2. Open LiftForge Content/Examples/Maps/LF_Showcase_Warm or LF_Showcase_Noir.
    3. Press Play. The map sets the LiftForge demo game mode for you.
    4. Walk to a car with W A S D and the mouse, aim at a hall button and press E (or the left mouse button). Press H to open the operator panel.

    Option B: place an elevator in your own level¶

    1. Open or create a level with a floor and enough room for a tall shaft. The elevator builds its own shaft, doors and landings.
    2. From LiftForge Content/Blueprints, drag BP_LF_Traction into the level. (Any BP_LF_<Key> works. See Presets and visual sets.)
    3. Select the actor. Under Elevator > Configuration you can see the Preset it uses. The floors, size, speed, doors and look all come from the preset.
    4. The actor origin is the reference for every floor: a floor's Elevation is its height above the actor origin, in centimetres. Move the actor so the lowest landing sits at the height of your floor.
    5. In World Settings, set GameMode Override to the LiftForge demo game mode (LFDemoGameMode). It spawns the sample desktop character. (If you use your own character, skip this step and read Using your own character.)
    6. Add a Player Start in front of the lowest landing and press Play.

    What you can do in play¶

    Action Desktop
    Move W A S D
    Look Mouse
    Press the button you aim at E or left mouse button
    Operator panel (modes, faults, inspection) H
    Switch between desktop and VR pawn V

    Press a hall button (up or down) to call the car. When the doors open, step in and press a floor button on the car operating panel. The car moves with a smooth acceleration and deceleration profile, levels at the landing, then opens the doors. They close again on their own after the dwell time.

    The top passenger floor in the demo building is restricted. Cabin buttons use access level 0, and the operator panel uses level 9. See Access levels in Bank and destination dispatch.

    Control it from a Blueprint¶

    Every way of operating a car goes through two functions on the elevator actor:

    • Execute Elevator Command: any command (a floor call, doors, a mode, a fault, an inspection toggle).
    • Call To Floor: a trip from a waiting floor to a destination.

    Example: in a level Blueprint, get a reference to the elevator and call Move Elevator To Floor with a floor id such as L2. The node continues on Arrived when the car is level with the floor and the doors are fully open. See Blueprint API.

    Next steps¶

    • Change size, speed or floors: Elevator Builder.
