    VR setup (OpenXR)¶

    LiftForge ships a sample VR pawn (LFVRPawn) for OpenXR. It has not been tested on a real VR headset. Only simulated checks were run: the pawn riding a car, its input assets and its settings. Treat it as a starting point. VR is optional: desktop use needs no headset and no OpenXR plugin.

    Enable OpenXR¶

    1. In Edit > Plugins, enable OpenXR (and the plugin for your headset runtime, if the runtime needs one), then restart the editor.
    2. Make sure a working OpenXR runtime is the active runtime on your PC (for example the one of your headset's software).
    3. Press Play > VR Preview, or use the V key in the demo to switch between the desktop character and the VR pawn.

    LiftForge itself needs no XR plugin (it uses the engine's Head Mounted Display module). The OpenXR plugin is a project decision.

    Controls¶

    Action Control
    Move Left thumbstick or trackpad (W A S D on the keyboard)
    Snap turn (30 degrees) Right thumbstick or trackpad X (Left and Right arrows)
    Press a button Right trigger (E on the keyboard), or touch the button with a controller
    Teleport Left trigger (T on the keyboard)
    Switch to desktop pawn V

    The OpenXR controller profile keys for Touch, Index, Vive and Windows Mixed Reality are mapped. You can remap everything; see Using your own character.

    Riding in VR¶

    LFVRPawn carries a LFRiderComponent. For your own VR pawn add a LFRiderComponent too, use a collidable root, and keep the camera and controllers below a tracking-origin component (the sample pawn calls it VROrigin). The rider component moves the pawn root with the cabin floor and leaves the local head and hand tracking alone.

    VR performance¶

    In Project Settings > Plugins > LiftForge Demo turn on Apply VR Performance CVars (off by default). While the sample VR pawn runs on a headset it then turns off dynamic global illumination and reflections (r.DynamicGlobalIlluminationMethod 0, r.ReflectionMethod 0), and restores your previous values when the pawn ends play. Frame timing on a real headset must be measured on the OpenXR runtime you target.

    The Laser Material setting in the same panel is the material of the VR pointer beam.

    Tips¶

    • The desktop operator panel (key H) is a desktop feature. In VR use the physical consoles.
    • If the pawn falls through the car, check that it has a collidable root and a LFRiderComponent (see FAQ).
    • Cabin mirrors are untested in VR. The mirror capture (LFMirrorCaptureComponent) renders one view from the camera's reflected position and has no per-eye handling, and it has not been run on a headset. On a VR project treat it as unsupported: switch off Mirror Capture on the preset to get the plain mirror finish.
