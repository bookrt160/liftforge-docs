    World Partition notes¶

    LiftForge is designed for World Partition levels, but has not been tested in one. A few rules keep banks working when cars and banks are in different cells.

    Link cars and bank by name, not by reference¶

    A hard actor reference from a bank to a car (or from a car to a bank) forces both actors to load together. Instead give the car and the bank the same Bank Name:

    1. Set Bank Name on the bank actor, for example North.
    2. Set the same Bank Name on each car.

    Cars and banks register with the world's LiftForge registry (LFElevatorRegistry, a world subsystem) when they begin play and unregister when they end play. With World Partition they therefore appear and disappear as their cells stream in, and find each other without hard references.

    • Calls are assigned to the cars that are loaded. A call assigned to a car that unloads is re-assigned on the next bank tick (every 0.1 s).
    • A bank with no loaded cars logs one warning and rejects calls (No Elevator Available).
    • Query the registry in Blueprints with Get Elevators, Get Bank Members, Find Bank and Get Banks on the LFElevatorRegistry subsystem.

    One car is one actor¶

    A car and its shaft, landings and doors are generated by a single actor. It loads and unloads as a whole. Treat it like any other large actor: decide in your project whether it should be spatially loaded, or placed in an always-loaded data layer if players must be able to call it from far away.

    Streaming of the car's own assets¶

    At BeginPlay a car loads the kit, the visual set and the materials it needs and keeps them resident, so the first build does not stall the game thread. Everything is referenced softly.

    Large buildings¶

    Hall stations, rail brackets, shaft lights, door tracks and frames repeat on every floor. When all of their materials have Used with Instanced Static Meshes enabled (all LiftForge masters and instances do), they are drawn as instanced meshes. See Performance tips.
